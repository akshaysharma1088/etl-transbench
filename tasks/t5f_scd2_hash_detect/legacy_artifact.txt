--     * NULL and the empty string give the same signature (not a change);
--     * trailing spaces are ignored; leading spaces and letter case are NOT;
--     * the delimiter is not escaped, so ('A|', 'B') and ('A', '|B') have the
--       same signature and are NOT a change.
