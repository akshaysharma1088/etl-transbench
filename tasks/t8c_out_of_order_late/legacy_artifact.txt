-- Semantics (idempotent late-arrival handling):
--   An event applies to a device ONLY IF (event_ts, event_seq) is STRICTLY
--   greater than the device's current (applied_event_ts, applied at load
--   time with event_seq unavailable for snapshot rows: compare event_ts >
--   applied_event_ts, OR event_ts == applied_event_ts is a REPLAY: skip).
--   Among surviving events per device, the latest by (event_ts, event_seq)
--   wins and sets reading + applied_event_ts.
--   LATE events (event_ts <= device's applied_event_ts) are DISCARDED even
--   though they may be newer than OTHER devices' watermarks — the watermark
--   is PER DEVICE, not global.
--   Devices with no surviving events carry forward. New devices apply their
--   latest event.
