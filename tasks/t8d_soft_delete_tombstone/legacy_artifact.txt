--         watermark (high_ts): events with event_ts <= high_ts already applied.

--   1. Keep events with event_ts > high_ts; apply per user SEQUENTIALLY by

--   3. op 'U' on an ACTIVE or new user: upsert email, status='ACTIVE',
--      status_ts=ts.

--      STRICTLY MORE than 7 days after status_ts of the deletion; otherwise
--      the update is DISCARDED (deletion is sticky inside the 7-day window).
-- Output: new_snapshot (user_id, email, status, status_ts).
